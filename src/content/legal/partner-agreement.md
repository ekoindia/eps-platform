A.3   Eko agrees to grant the Partner access to EPS on the terms below. The Parties therefore agree as follows.

## Frequently Asked Questions

> **Not part of the Agreement.** This FAQ is provided only to help you understand the
> sample above. It has no contractual effect and is not legal advice. If anything here
> differs from the Agreement text, the Agreement text prevails.

### Does signing this Agreement commit me to pay a fee?

No. Signing does not, by itself, make any amount payable. The Agreement sets out the terms
that apply *if and when* you actually use EPS — the fees, pricing and commission in Clause
3.3 attach to the transactions you put through the platform.

### When is the one-time integration fee charged?

Only after you have actually integrated the EPS APIs, gone live in production, and started
using them. Nothing is charged for signing this Agreement, for sandbox/UAT access, or for
evaluating the APIs. (Clause 10.4 states that the activation fee is non-refundable — that
applies once such a fee has actually become due, not to the act of signing.)

### Am I obliged to use Eko's services after signing?

No. There is no minimum usage, minimum volume or minimum revenue commitment in this
Agreement. You may sign, evaluate the platform, and choose never to go live.

### Can I opt out later?

Yes. You can stop using the services at any time, and either party may terminate for
convenience on thirty (30) days' prior written notice (Clause 10.2). On termination you
settle any dues actually incurred and stop using Eko's platform and branding (Clause 10.3).
Note that some obligations deliberately outlive termination — confidentiality for two years
(Clause 6.1), the indemnities in Clause 8, and the non-solicitation / non-compete in
Clause 11.1 for twelve months.

### Is the arrangement exclusive?

Eko's side is expressly non-exclusive — it may appoint other partners, including in your
territory (Clause 2.2). On your side, Clause 11.1 restricts you from running a directly
competing business in the territory during the Term and for twelve months afterwards, and
from soliciting Eko's employees, agents or customers, unless Eko consents in writing. If
that clause is a problem for your business, raise it before signing.

### Will I be able to read the actual agreement before I sign it?

Yes. The final agreement is presented to you in full, on screen, to read and review before
you accept it. Nothing is signed on your behalf, and no clause takes effect until you
explicitly agree.

### Is this the final contract?

No. This is a sample published for your review. The commercials (pricing, commission
rates), the schedules referred to in the Agreement, and your own legal-entity details are
settled separately and communicated in writing.

Write to <eps@eko.in>, marked for the Compliance Desk (Clause 13.3).
